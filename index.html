00 — export csv from excel
⚠ Before you begin — make sure your OpenAir timesheet has all project rows expanded and that the Client : Engagement and Task columns are filled in. The script can only fill in hours and notes; it cannot expand rows or populate project info for you.
  • Open your Excel timesheet Go to the worksheet tab containing the data you want to submit in OpenAir this week.
  • Save a CSV copy Give the file a new name, set the format to CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited) (*.csv), and save it somewhere convenient.
  • Click OK on the warning Excel will tell you that only the active sheet will be saved — that's expected.
  • Your CSV is ready Drag and drop it into the upload area below.

how to use
  • Drag the bookmarklet Drag it to your browser's bookmarks bar. You'll need to do this each week after uploading a new CSV.
  • Open your OpenAir timesheet In another tab, navigate to the week you want to fill.
  • Click the bookmark Fields will auto-fill with delays to handle the notes dialogs.
  • Review and submit The script does not submit — you stay in control.
